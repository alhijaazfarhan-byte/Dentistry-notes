Dental Plaque

Dental plaque is a structured community of microorganisms that develops on tooth surfaces. Good oral hygiene helps control plaque accumulation.

Dental Caries

Dental caries is a biofilm-mediated disease that can cause progressive destruction of mineralized tooth tissues.
